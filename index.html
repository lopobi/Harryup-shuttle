Driver's Contract / Employment Agreement

1. Job Title

The Driver is employed as a Shuttle Driver to transport passengers safely and professionally.

2. Main Duty - Safety of Passengers

The Driver is FULLY RESPONSIBLE for the safety of ALL passengers at ALL times.

  1. a) Every passenger must arrive safely at his/her destination
  2. b) Correct pickup and drop-off points must be followed
  3. c) Passengers are safe during the whole trip - from pickup to drop-off
  4. d) Drive carefully, obey road rules, do not speed
  5. e) Do NOT drive under influence of alcohol or drugs
  6. f) Check vehicle before every trip - brakes, tyres, lights, fuel, water, oil

3. Responsibility

Driver is responsible for accidents, fines, vehicle damage, delays and breakdowns caused by negligence.

4. Passenger Care

Treat passengers with respect. Help elderly/children/disabled. Seatbelts on. No overload. No smoking/drinking/fighting.

5. Working Rules

Be on time. Follow route and schedule. Report before/after trip. No unauthorised money collection.

6. Disciplinary Action

Warning / Suspension / Fine / Immediate Termination for breaking this contract.

7. Declaration

I, the Driver, confirm I have read and understood this contract. I accept full responsibility for the safety of passengers and everything that happens during the trip. Safety of passengers is my number one priority.
